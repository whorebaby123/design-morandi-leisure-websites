# Cool Coast Desktop

Use this mode for an airy, meditative personal homepage with a gentle retro-computing structure.

## Palette

Recommended starting set:

| Role | Color | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Atmospheric base | `#8D9DA2` | foggy mineral blue-gray |
| Pale surface | `#D6DAD3` | window and menu chrome |
| Structural ink | `#283337` | text, borders, hard shadows |
| Lavender accent | `#AAA5B4` | image field or secondary module |
| Sand accent | `#D7CAA8` | secondary action and note surface |
| Rose accent | `#D9B6AF` | primary playback action |

Allow small adjustments for content imagery and accessibility, but retain low saturation and the cool/warm tension between blue-gray and sand/rose.

## Composition

- Use a slim, full-width system menu bar as the top anchor.
- Reserve a large open field for one oversized editorial statement.
- Layer two to four bordered windows at different sizes and modest depths.
- Let one main window hold the central function; use smaller windows as forecast, note, status, archive, or contextual modules.
- Overlap intentionally, while keeping every important control readable. The composition should feel arranged on a desktop, not scattered randomly.
- A narrow status strip may sit near the lower edge and show time, mode, signal, or location.

Desktop may use absolute positioning for art direction. At tablet width, reflow modules into a deliberate vertical sequence and remove decorative rotations.

## Typography and geometry

- Use a very large, tightly tracked sentence-case heading with compact line height.
- Keep functional labels readable at 12–14 px; use small uppercase tracked text only for secondary Latin metadata.
- Use mostly rectangular windows with square or nearly square corners.
- Window title bars should be thin and information-dense; small circular indicators are allowed as chrome details.

## Materials

- Opaque gray-green window surfaces with 2 px dark outlines.
- Hard shadows around 6–9 px for windows, slightly smaller for buttons.
- Abstract horizon, ripple, haze, or light-disc fields may create coastal atmosphere without pretending to be photography.
- Texture should be subtle enough that body text stays clean.

## Interaction signature

- A raised square play button is the strongest control.
- A waveform may animate only while playing.
- Menu labels may bring a window forward or briefly lift it.
- Notes may use a simple keep/saved toggle.

## Avoid

- filling the screen with equally sized app cards;
- making every window translucent;
- nostalgic pixel typography;
- strong cyan or tropical blue;
- turning the layout into a literal clone of a desktop operating system.
