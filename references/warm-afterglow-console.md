# Warm Afterglow Console

Use this mode for a more expressive, graphic personal homepage with poster-scale type and tactile broadcast controls.

## Palette

Recommended starting set:

| Role | Color | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Atmospheric base | `#C5AAA0` | dusty shell pink |
| Pale surface | `#E0D6C8` | control panel and readable field |
| Structural ink | `#4F4142` | text, dividers, hard shadows |
| Sage accent | `#92A092` | secondary action and tuning state |
| Terracotta accent | `#C98980` | primary playback and live state |
| Butter accent | `#D2C4A8` | poster field and highlights |

Use large flat color relationships. Avoid gradients that make the palette glossy or synthetic.

## Composition

- Split the page asymmetrically: approximately 55–62% editorial poster and 38–45% functional console.
- The poster side should carry one oversized message, one strong geometric motif, and sparse issue/edition metadata.
- The console side should be denser, vertically sequenced, and divided by dark rules rather than generic cards.
- Finish with a full-width narrow ticker or broadcast strip.
- Use strong edges between zones. The page should feel like a designed cover attached to a piece of equipment.

On mobile, stack the poster above the console. Preserve a generous poster height so the display type still feels intentional.

## Typography and geometry

- Use bold uppercase display type with very tight tracking and compressed line height.
- One phrase may be outlined, cream-filled, or obliqued to create poster tension.
- Combine large numerals, compact secondary metadata, and comfortably readable functional titles.
- Use circles for orbit marks, records, and tuning indicators; keep action buttons rectangular and heavy.

## Materials

- Flat printed pigments, thick rules, hard shadows, and a light paper grain.
- Concentric circles or cropped arcs can suggest sun, record, signal, or broadcast range.
- The console should resemble physical equipment through alignment, scales, ticks, and control grouping—not photorealistic skeuomorphism.

## Interaction signature

- Use a wide terracotta play/pause control paired with a smaller sage skip control.
- A record or dial may rotate slowly while playing.
- Mood or channel selectors should form one compact segmented row with a clearly inverted active state.
- A ticker may move continuously, but slowly, with duplicated content for a seamless loop.

## Avoid

- reusing the cool mode's floating-window composition;
- soft glass panels or pastel SaaS cards;
- decorative circles with no relationship to broadcast, time, or signal;
- excessive rounded corners;
- shrinking the poster typography until it reads like an ordinary landing-page hero.
